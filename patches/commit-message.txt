Headunit Server: join WiFi before the WiFi button's search

The WiFi button's one-shot search probes this unit's own subnet. A unit
that has not joined the phone's hotspot yet has no subnet to probe, so
the search ended at once and the user had to press again. Android 10
units are the slow case: the platform's reconnect scans back off while
nothing is connected, so a saved hotspot can sit in range for a minute.

The background search loop has the same blind spot: every 10 s it
sweeps whatever interface is up, whether or not the unit is on WiFi.

With "Join WiFi before searching" on (Wireless Connection, Headunit
Server, Auto; on by default), while the unit is not on WiFi the loop
parks instead of sweeping, asks Android for WiFi scans, and resumes the
moment a WiFi network is joined - so the phone is found at startup
without pressing anything. The WiFi button:
- searches at once, as before, when the unit is already on WiFi;
- otherwise asks Android to scan for WiFi, paced to the foreground
  budget of four scans in two minutes (WifiJoinWaitPolicy), and waits
  for a WiFi network, up to the "WiFi join timeout" (10-120 s, 30 s);
- shows the wait on the home card as "Preparing the network", and uses
  the pill rather than the overlay, whose 30 s bound could end mid-wait;
- switches WiFi on below Android 10, or opens the WiFi panel above it;
- searches once the join settles, or once anyway on timeout.

A second press while waiting keeps the wait; the status pill's X ends it.

Automatic connection (Headunit Server, Auto; on by default):
- a start delay (0-60 s, 15 s) counted down on the home card with
  Connect now / Cancel; opening settings stops it, leaving restarts it;
  off, only the WiFi button connects;
- the card says what the search waits for: the phone's WiFi, a hotspot
  not in range, another network, "WiFi connected, connecting to the
  phone", or the phone's head unit server being off (port 5277 refused
  rather than unanswered);
- the first sweep the moment WiFi is joined (0.5 s), then every 5 s
  for a minute instead of the loop's 10 s, still one sweep at a time;
- the phone's network and address learned from the last session; a
  refusing gateway that is the known phone skips the subnet sweep;
- the home screen's "last session" auto-connect no longer dials the
  phone's last address beside the search;
- one "Timeline:" line per connection in the log.
Both settings are carried in the settings backup.
